# Changelog

## 4.0.0 (2026-10-27)

The skill now teaches every job eonik's server does, rendered from the server's own guides and
tool list rather than written by hand:

- working on one creative: look, change, undo, compare and make versions;
- making a new ad from a script the person approves once, and recreating an ad with the brand's
  product, person or place;
- answering the client's notes and checking a finished package against the media plan;
- bringing a brand in from its handover files, and reading what assistants did for it.

Examples describe the work ("the spring master", "the launch set", "the client") and name no
customer.

## 3.0.0 (2026-10-10)

The skill makes adapts with eonik, and answers brand and research questions.
